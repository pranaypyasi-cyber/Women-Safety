About Suraksha

One simple place for safety information when you need it fast.

Why we made this

When someone feels unsafe, searching for the right number or advice wastes time. Helplines are scattered across many websites, and not everyone knows which number to call for which problem.

Suraksha brings the important things together on one light, mobile-friendly website: who to call, what to do, and who to reach first.

What you can do here

Find a helpline

Search emergency, women, cyber, health, legal and child helplines, and call with one tap.

Open my contacts

Your privacy

Suraksha has no login and no server. The contacts you add are stored only in your own browser. We do not collect, see or share them. If you clear your browser data, they will be removed.

How it is built

A beginner-friendly stack, with no database needed.

  • HTML for the structure of each page
  • CSS for the design and mobile layout
  • JavaScript for search, filters and saving contacts
  • JSON files to store the helplines and tips
  • localStorage to remember your contacts

Please note

Suraksha is an information portal and not an emergency service. It cannot send help or track your location. If you are in danger, call 112 immediately. Helpline numbers can change, so please verify them on official government websites.
